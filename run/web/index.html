Worth knowing

    Replenishments of size Q are placed whenever inventory position (on hand + on order − backorders) falls to r. Over one time unit the policy costs

    Y(Q, r) = h(Q/2 + r − Dl) + AD/Q + pDn(r)/Q + Dv

    where n(r) is the demand expected to go unmet in one cycle. Setting both partial derivatives to zero leaves two equations that have to hold together:

    Q = √(2D[A + p·n(r)] / h) (1)
    1 − G(r) = Qh / (pD) (2)

    Neither can be solved alone, so the method starts at the EOQ, reads r off (2), computes n(r), and returns to (1) for a new Q, until r stops moving.

    Policy 2 — periodic review (R, S)

    Every T the stock position is raised to S. An order placed at one review arrives a lead time L later, and nothing more can be ordered until the next review, so S has to cover the demand over the protection interval T + L, written XT+L. Per time unit the policy costs

    Y2(S) = A/T + h(S − DL − DT/2) + p·n(S)/T + Dv

    where G, g and n now belong to XT+L. Setting the derivative in S to zero gives

    1 − G(S) = hT / p (3)

    Before the iteration The problem states a carrying charge and demand per period, not h and the distribution over the lead time. Each is built first.
